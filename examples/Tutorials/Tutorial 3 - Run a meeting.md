# Tutorial 3: Run a meeting
*10 minutes · ← [[Tutorial 2 - Finish, cancel or reopen a task]] · next: [[Tutorial 4 - Weekly review]]*

**You'll learn:** before, during and after a meeting, and how requirements changed in it get their history.

## Before
1. On [[00 Home]] (or a hub) press **Meeting** (preset **Alt+M**).
2. Pick **2025 Crash Data Load**, title `County codes follow-up`, a date later this week, attendees **Marcus Bell** and **Rita Gomez**.
3. **See:** it's under *Upcoming meetings* on Home and the hub, and on both people's pages.

## During
4. In the new meeting, write notes under **Notes** (Editing view: Ctrl+E; that's fine for free text).
5. An action item came up? Press **Quick capture** at the top of the meeting → **Task**. It lands in the project's
   tasks *and* is linked to this meeting.
6. A requirement changed? Press **Change requirement** at the top of the meeting → pick
   **CDL-REQ-001 County codes validated** → what changed and why.

## See this
- [[CDL-REQ-001 County codes validated]] → **Version history** has a new row, linked to your meeting and its attendees.
- The meeting lists the requirements it changed.
- New requirement instead? **Requirement** button at the top of the meeting; it's linked the same way.
